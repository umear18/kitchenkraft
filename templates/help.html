🆘 Help & Support

How does the ingredient matching work?

Select what you have in "My Kitchen". Kitchen Kraft compares your list with every recipe's ingredient list and shows a match percentage — 100% means you can cook it right now, and anything lower shows exactly what you're missing.

Are the nutrition values exact?

Nutrition values are estimated from standard ingredient quantities stored in our database. Treat them as a helpful guide rather than a lab-tested measurement.

Can I save recipes for later?

Yes — tap the heart icon on any recipe card or recipe detail page to add it to your Favorites.

Where can I see what I've cooked before?

Visit "Cooking History" from the sidebar to see every recipe you've marked as cooked, along with the date.

About Kitchen Kraft

Kitchen Kraft is an ingredient-based recipe recommendation and health & nutrition web application, built as a final-year CSE project.
